Origin Story — Lagos 2023

In 2023, I visited my Mum's book supply business in Lagos. Inventory was in disarray.

Customers paid for items that were out of stock — embarrassing refund.
No way to know what to restock without a manual shelf walk.
Calculator punching, manual invoicing mistakes, losing money daily.

I built her a tracker to stop chaos.

Then new users came — pharmacies, tile distributors, salesmen, marketers. Their requests built WROSYS.



Founder Innovation — Voice Invoicing, July 2026

Say "Maths textbook 10, Confo inhaler 5" → 2 sec invoice, stock deducted, out-of-stock blocked.

It was not requested. I invented it to kill typing during rush.
It is appreciated by all — pharmacies, distributors, book suppliers, salesmen love it.


Features — Built From Real Requests

- Voice Invoicing — Founder innovation July 2026. Say product + qty, invoice in 2 secs
- Block Selling Out-Of-Stock — Mum's original pain. Stops embarrassing refunds
- Low Stock Alerts — Know what to restock without manual shelf walk. Requested by pharmacies
- Expiration Date Tracker — Track drug expiry, avoid selling expired stock. Requested by pharmacies
- Barcode Scanner — Scan to add to cart instantly. Requested by pharmacies
- Owner Dashboard Analytics — Sales & Profit TODAY / Yesterday / 7d / 30d / 6mo bar chart. Requested by tile distributors
- Supplier Accounts — Track supplier balances, purchase orders. Requested by tile distributors
- Customer Accounts & Credit — Track credit sales, payments, balances. Requested by salesmen
- WhatsApp PDF Invoices — Share invoice PDF via WhatsApp instantly after sale. Requested by salesmen & marketers
